## 2. Deliberately NOT applied — disclosed, not silent

    - fastapi `Request` import and the X-Telegram-* header fallback.
      Identity comes from the BODY only. On production this is inert anyway:
      `consumption_operations` does not exist, so schema_capabilities reports
      identity_table=False and has_identity is forced False.
    - the `require_model_writer_allowed` dependency. The live route is UNGATED
      and Shape A must not add a gate. The deployed decorator is byte-identical
      to the pre-deploy one.
    - the `correct_log` / `delete_log` endpoints. They are part of ea17659 but
      are outside Shape A (they arrive with Shape B). Shipping a new endpoint
      that can soft-delete production rows was not sanctioned, so it was not
      shipped. `log_intents.correct_log/delete_log` exist but are unreachable.
    - `models.py`: NOT changed at all. The ea17659 models.py delta is
      COMMENT-ONLY, and `deleted_at` is deliberately NOT ORM-mapped. Mapping it
      (`deferred=True`) was tried and rejected during development: it still
      emitted `deleted_at` in the INSERT, and with a server_default it then
      emitted `RETURNING measurements.deleted_at`, which 500s on this schema.
      The guard lives in log_intents.schema_capabilities() instead.
      Verified in the running worker: `deleted_at mapped: False`.

    => The deployed image is NOT byte-identical to ea17659. It is production +
       ea17659's logging delta, minus the three items above.
